About the emulators

All emulators except xemu and RPCS3 are libretro cores. ARMSX2 and FAKE-08 aren't built by libretro, so Romlin downloads them from Romlin's forks, which build them for each platform. Dolphin, PPSSPP, Flycast, Beetle PSX HW, Mupen64Plus-Next and the PS2 emulators draw through Vulkan when the computer has a working Vulkan driver, and through OpenGL otherwise. On macOS, Vulkan comes from the bundled MoltenVK. Some systems need BIOS files, which Romlin takes from your RomM server's firmware. PS3 games need the PS3 system software (PS3UPDAT.PUP) in RomM's PS3 firmware, and play from a game folder or an ISO, or a zip or 7z of one.

Romlin doesn't include any games or BIOS files. Games come from your RomM server, and BIOS files from its firmware.
